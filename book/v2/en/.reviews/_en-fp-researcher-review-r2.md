# EN FP/type-theory researcher review — r2 (diff b1e59ad..HEAD)

Lens: an outside FP / type-theory researcher (TAPL level). Scope: English text changed since the
last EN review (`git diff b1e59ad..HEAD -- book/v2/en`), read together with the surrounding
sections. I checked claims about real Rigor against `/Users/megurine/repo/ruby/rigor` (ADRs and
`lib/`), and where it settled a question I ran real Rigor (`exe/rigor check` / `type-of` on scratch
files) and `exe/chibirigor`. Honest simplification is not counted as an error.

## Headline verdict

**Mostly sound. Fix two ERRORs before shipping.** The r2 rewrite of Part 4 (cycle detection as an
exact stop on the type-*building* side, kept apart from `seen`; fuel as the outer safety net that
falls back to `bound`) is correct and checks out against `hkt_reducer.rb`. So do the Part 5 changes:
join rather than unification, the observed union as a lower bound used "for precision only"
(ADR-67 WD1/WD6b), and `--params=observed` versus `untyped` (ADR-14 WD2). The Part 6 updates
(dynamic_origin as closure-escape provenance, ADR-56 write-back) and the a1/a5 and Little 5/6/9
updates on open/closed shapes and unreachable branches also match real Rigor.

The weak spot is the new "real Rigor answers `:yes` with `Dynamic`" layer. It holds only when a
**whole side** is `Dynamic`, or when `App` is the **expected** side. Read naturally, the book
generalizes it to "anything involving `untyped`/`App` passes quietly", and real Rigor contradicts
that. I reproduced both cases as actual `call.argument-type-mismatch` errors. Little Part 9 already
states the correct behavior (`1 | Dynamic[Top]` can be `:no`), so the volumes now disagree with each
other.
